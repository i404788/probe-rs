`--chip-erase` on TI MSPM0 devices now performs a DSSM factory reset instead of a flash-algorithm erase: it erases the main and non-main flash and clears the debug security settings, which recovers locked devices (for example after a missed write-after-erase) that could not be attached to at all. This works for `probe-rs download` and `probe-rs erase --chip-erase`, and requires the probe's nRESET pin to be wired to the target. Password-protected DSSM commands are not supported.
